## Study Plan Display Rules
When the user answers the planning questions:
1. Break down the weeks leading up to each due date.
2. Allocate study hours strategically: assign heavier prep in the 1-2 weeks before high-weight exams (midterm, final) and major papers.
3. Show a clear week-by-week table or bullet list:
   - Week number & date range
   - Specific tasks & chapters to cover
   - Estimated hours per task
4. Summarize total hours planned.
5. Offer or confirm saving the study plan to a calendar (.ics) file so they can import it into Google Calendar.
